New-hire training: handling a billing complaint
Session length 90 minutes. Trainer: Dr. Priya Shah. Backup contact priya.shah@harborpay.com or 628-555-0177.

Outcomes from the workbook, page 4:
- Name the three steps: listen, restate, offer the next step.
- Practice them on the two sample calls in the packet (Call A late fee, Call B duplicate charge).
- you cannot approve a refund on this call.

Run of show:
- Welcome and read the outcome lines aloud.
- Walk Call A together. I play the customer. They practice "listen, restate, offer the next step."
- Pairs on Call B. Switch roles halfway.
- One pair performs. Group names which step they heard.
- Write one sentence they will say when they cannot approve a refund. Share two, no more.
- Close: reread the three steps. Remind them the packet stays at the desk.

Skip the section on chargebacks. That is week 2.
