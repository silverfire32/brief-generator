Lesson: Equivalent fractions, grade 4
Session: 40 minutes.
Room 12, Thursday
Teacher: Ms. Alvarez (alvarez.m@riverside.k12.edu). If a sub calls, use 510-555-0142.

Aim: students explain why 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 using fraction strips.

Flow:
- Settle and show the strip for 1/2. Ask "what do you notice?"
- Pairs fold strips to find another fraction equal to 1/2. They must write the pair, like 1/2 and 2/4.
- Gallery: three pairs share. Record only what they say on the board. Phrase for the board: "same amount, different name."
- Exit ticket: "Write two fractions equal to 1/2 and say how you know." No helping during the ticket.
- Collect tickets. Do not give the answer key.

Don't: skip ahead to decimals. Don't call on the same three students. Student: Maya Chen already presented yesterday, so invite someone else.

Materials: paper strips, one marker, exit slip half-sheet.
If time is short, cut the gallery to two shares.
